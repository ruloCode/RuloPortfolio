From November 12 to 15, 2026, I took part in the **[2026 Epic AI MegaJam](https://itch.io/jam/2026-epic-ai-megajam)**, the jam Epic Games hosted on itch.io. We were a team of five, and we came out with **Director's Dream**, a 2.5D run-and-gun made in Unreal Engine.

## The theme: Director's Cut

The theme was **Director's Cut**: your vision, your edits, your final cut. And if you use AI agents, you direct those too. You could enter solo or in teams of up to five, with Unreal Engine or UEFN, and any AI tools were optional. We had four days to go from an idea to a game someone else could download and finish.

## What we made

In Director's Dream every level is a hand-built film set on a toy diorama, and the Director won't yell "CUT!" until you survive the take. There are four sets, each with its own boss: The Village, The Mountain, The Samurai Castle and Dinolandia. It has three difficulties and an original soundtrack composed by the team, and it plays with keyboard and mouse or a gamepad.

![The Samurai Castle set in Director's Dream: cherry blossoms, a pagoda, a torii gate and lanterns](/images/projects/directors-dream/samurai-castle.jpg)

## My part

My work on the team covered five areas: agent setup, project management, QA, 3D modeling and level refinement.

## How we disclosed AI

On the itch.io page we disclose AI use as **AI Assisted** for code, graphics, sounds and text. Third-party credits are the ones that page lists: assets from Fab, animations from Mixamo (Adobe) and the font Lilita One.

## What I'm taking with me

- **You direct an agent the way you direct a team.** Clear instructions and a tight scope get more done than asking for everything at once.
- **Human QA doesn't get delegated.** Agents speed up production, but someone has to play the game and decide what is ready and what isn't.
- **Under a deadline, judgment means cutting.** Shipping something complete and playable is worth more than a bigger scope left unfinished.
