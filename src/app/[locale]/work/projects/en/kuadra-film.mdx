## Overview

**Kuadra Film** is a commercial photo and video studio in Bogotá. I built its public site and, behind the login, the portal its clients use.

## The Site

A studio site built to turn visits into quote requests.

- Bold editorial typography and a portfolio of 100+ pieces.
- Services, process and tailored proposal pages, each with its own share image.
- Every path ends in one action: get a quote.

## The Portal

- An editorial calendar and deliverables per client.
- A review flow and a production board.

## How It's Built

Next.js and TypeScript on Supabase, with smooth-scroll motion and a mobile-first layout.
