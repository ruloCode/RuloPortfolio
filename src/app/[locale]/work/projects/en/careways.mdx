## Overview

**CareWays Collaborative** trains pediatric intensive care teams in resource-limited settings. As **Tech Lead**, from January to July 2026, I built its platform end to end: education, telesimulation and clinical data in one place.

## The Problem

Training, practice and outcomes lived apart. A team took a course in one tool, rehearsed a hard case over a video call and tracked patients in a spreadsheet, so nobody could connect what was taught with what happened at the bedside.

## What It Does

- **Courses** with video, pre and post tests, and automatic certificates.
- **Live telesimulation**: an educator runs a clinical scenario and scores the team in real time.
- **A patient registry** with PIM3 mortality risk and outcome dashboards.

## How It's Built

- **Next.js 16 and React 19** on Oracle Autonomous Database, with hand-written SQL.
- **Role-based access** for clinicians, educators, champions and admins.
- **A sandbox mode**, so practice data never mixes with real records.
- **Cloudflare Stream** video with dubbed Spanish audio; the interface is in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
