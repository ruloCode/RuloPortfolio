## Overview

**MG Company Group** develops artists in Bogotá and Cartagena. I built both halves: the site that shows the artists, and the admin panel that runs the business behind them.

## The Public Site

- Artists, projects, gallery and events.
- An open call that brings in new talent.
- Events with QR ticket check-in at the door.

## The Admin Panel

- Artist roster, shared inbox, calendar and meetings.
- Accounts receivable and team workload.
- Social media and event management.

## How It's Built

Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind, with Supabase for auth and Postgres, and transactional email through Resend.
