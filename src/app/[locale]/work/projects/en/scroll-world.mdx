## Overview

The home of this site is not a page you scroll: it is a world you fly through. Scrolling pilots a camera across an AI-generated papercraft diorama, using the same scrubbing technique behind Apple's product pages.

## What It Does

- Five scenes and four aerial connectors, stitched so the flight never cuts.
- Each scene carries one idea of the site, and the copy arrives as the camera lands.

## How It's Built

- **Vanilla JS, no dependencies**: blob-loaded clips, `requestAnimationFrame` scrubbing and crossfades between clips.
- **Three encodes per clip** (AV1, HEVC and H.264) at two sizes, so each device downloads the lightest one it can play.
- **iOS priming and mobile hardening**, because a phone will not seek a video it has not been allowed to play.
- **Reduced-motion support and WCAG AA contrast**: without motion, the scenes become stills.

## Stack

Next.js, vanilla JavaScript, video scrubbing and AI-generated video.
