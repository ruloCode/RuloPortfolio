## Overview

**Director's Dream** is a 2.5D run-and-gun shot like a movie: every level is a hand-built film set on a toy diorama, and the Director won't yell "CUT!" until you survive the take. A team of five of us built it in Unreal Engine for the [2026 Epic AI MegaJam](https://itch.io/jam/2026-epic-ai-megajam), between November 12 and 15, 2026. You can download it and play it on Windows from [itch.io](https://celismx.itch.io/directors-dream).

![Director's Dream key art: the heroine with a bazooka facing a dragon and a mechanical dinosaur, under film-set lighting, with the game's title](/images/projects/directors-dream/key-art.jpg)

## The Challenge

Epic Games hosted the jam on itch.io, with the theme **Director's Cut**: your vision, your edits, your final cut. And if you use AI agents, you direct those too. You could enter solo or in teams of up to five, with Unreal Engine or UEFN, and any AI tools were optional. We had four days to go from an idea to a game someone else could download and finish.

## What We Built

A single-player game with four sets, each with its own boss:

- The Village
- The Mountain
- The Samurai Castle
- Dinolandia

You shoot in 8 directions, double jump, slide, dodge, throw grenades and use a knife up close. Shield troopers go down when you aim for the face. There is a cinematic intro, world transitions and an ending, three difficulties (we recommend Easy) and an original soundtrack composed by the team. It plays with keyboard and mouse or a gamepad.

## Results

- A playable Windows prototype, published on [itch.io](https://celismx.itch.io/directors-dream).
- Four sets, each with its own boss.
- Submitted before the deadline to a jam that received 2,782 games. Here is [our entry](https://itch.io/jam/2026-epic-ai-megajam/rate/5377882).

The game is designed as a single-player experience; online co-op does not work in this version.

## Learnings

- **You direct an agent the way you direct a team.** Clear instructions and a tight scope get more done than asking for everything at once.
- **Human QA doesn't get delegated.** Agents speed up production, but someone has to play the game and decide what is ready and what isn't.
- **Under a deadline, judgment means cutting.** Shipping something complete and playable is worth more than a bigger scope left unfinished.

It is the same work I do in applied AI: directing agents, reviewing what they produce and shipping on time.
